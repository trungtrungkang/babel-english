# Experiments and feedback log

## Meeting decision record

For each item record: lesson/activity; keep/change/remove; evidence or reason; unknowns; confirming person; action owner; next test and review date. Leave approval and outcomes blank before the meeting. Observe task comprehension, actual speaking, support needed and whether teachers have enough evidence to review.


## Method

Test one main hypothesis per iteration. Observe before explaining. A small sample finds usability problems and needs; it does not prove programme effectiveness. The criteria below are proposals, not approved targets or measured results.

| Hypothesis | Trial | Desired signal | Response if unsuccessful |
|---|---|---|---|
| Children know what to do | Ask 5–8 children to open a task without coaching | Most enter the right lesson with at most one hint | Improve entry/instructions before adding games |
| A short journey fits learners | Observe duration, stopping points and help | Suitable duration and willingness to speak | Remove repetition, split the lesson or clarify instructions |
| Learners answer independently | Change details and reduce models | Transfer beyond repeating memorised text | Adjust support/difficulty rather than rewards alone |
| Teachers can sustain it | Review a batch of recordings | Within Babel’s agreed minutes per class | Improve queue/rubric or review fewer tasks |
| Parents understand reports | Ask what was learned, completed and needs help | Answers in about 30 seconds | Simplify labels and status |
| Vic/Châu can edit content | Edit and publish a lesson unaided | No code changes needed | Improve templates before importing everything |

## Measuring learning during the pilot

Use comparable before/after tasks plus a new situation. Use the same rubric and cross-review or conceal assessment timing where appropriate. Track attempts and hint use; an open tab is not speaking time.

Distinguish unfinished work, technical submission failures and learning difficulties. Track microphone/upload issues, devices, waiting time and teacher effort. Improvement in an uncontrolled group is not proof that the software caused it.

## Session record template

- Date and observer
- Demo version, lesson and device
- Role/age group without identifying a child
- Main hypothesis and task
- Observed behaviour, separated from interpretation
- Exact quotations only where permission allows
- Difficult steps and assistance required
- Technical problems
- What the participant would keep
- Proposed change and severity: blocking / difficult / improvement
- Decision, owner and next trial date
