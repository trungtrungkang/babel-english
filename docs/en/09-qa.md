## Still to verify

- Real microphone recording, playback, submission and replay in teacher/parent views on the presentation device. The user’s microphone was not enabled automatically.
- Synthetic voice availability/quality across Chrome, Safari, iOS and Android.
- Denied permission, full storage, closing during recording, the 60-second limit and actual codec support.
- Feedback and audio persistence after a real submission and reload.
- Real Babel videos, which have not been supplied.
- Physical phones/tablets: viewport simulation is not hardware testing.
- Full mouse navigation: automation clicks in the embedded browser were inconsistent; keyboard activation worked.
- Reset confirmation and return to clean state.
- Learning effectiveness, age suitability, teacher workload and multi-learner load: no pilot yet.

## Known limits

One learner, six lessons, one current result per lesson; no authentication, server storage or AI. Browser state may contain v2 content and progress created during QA. Use Content → Reset demo data for a fresh demonstration.

The project-documentation addition is tracked in the Vietnamese QA source and verified separately; no new claims about audio or learning effectiveness follow from adding the library.
