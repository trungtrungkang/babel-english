# Start here — Châu & Vic

## Purpose of the meeting

Review whether the learning approach fits Babel, how materials should be authored and whether the teacher workflow is practical. This is a discussion prototype, not an approved curriculum or a system ready for real learners.

## A 10–15 minute walkthrough

1. **Lesson 1 — At the ice cream shop:** open Practise, choose a flavour and role-play three turns with Bo. Move from Full model to Keywords to No hints. Bo follows a script and does not understand the recording.
2. **Lesson 2 — At the fruit market:** use a shopping list to request each item. Switch lists; try the final challenge with different quantities. Spoken tick marks are self-tracking, not assessment.
3. **Lesson 3 — My little shopping trip:** choose the order of picture cards as an outline; tell 3–4 sentences and remove support. Cards use symbolic illustrations, not finished Babel artwork.
4. Read “I can…” and expand Goal and assessment. In Teacher, read the suggested classroom connection.
5. To try the feedback loop, an adult can record and submit the final answer. Review task meaning, intelligibility and independence, then open Parent. Completion preview works without a microphone but creates no submission.
6. Open Learning and content design and the Demonstration guide to discuss the approach.

Lessons 4–6 illustrate possible expansion; they have not received the same editorial treatment. The six-stage navigation remains a prototype convention, not a mandatory final curriculum format.

## Known requirements

- Supplement classroom teaching, with Speaking first.
- Learner, teacher, parent and content-management views.
- Vietnamese/English support across device types.
- Foundations that support additional skills later.

## Assumptions to validate

Ages 8–11, the Explorers 1 label, 5–8 minute practice sessions and 8–10 minute cumulative sessions, language selection and lesson volume. There is no CEFR mapping, measured learning outcome or feedback from real learners yet.

## Five decisions to make

| Decision | Question | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| First audience | Which ages and level should trial it? | One defined learner group |
| Learning goal | What should learners do by the end, and know beforehand? | An observable outcome |
| Materials | Which Babel unit, videos and transcripts fit? | 3–5 authorised source materials |
| Assessment | What counts as sufficient meaning, clarity and independence? | Criteria with examples |
| Operations | Who approves and reviews, and how many minutes per class? | Named owners and a workload limit |

## Capture feedback

For each feature record **keep / change / remove**, evidence or observed behaviour, the confirming person and the next validation step. Templates are in Experiments and Decision log. This library is read-only; conclusions can be added to source documents after the meeting.

## What actually works?

Navigation, task selections, per-lesson progress, local recording/submission, feedback entry and basic content edits. Real recording still needs direct verification on the presentation device.

Babel videos, speech understanding/scoring, real sign-in, multi-user synchronisation and proven educational effectiveness are not included. Do not use real children’s data in this prototype.
